01

Research Interests

Theory, imaging, and computation for inverse problems.

I

Inverse boundary value problems

Inverse problems for nonlinear wave equations, viscoelastic Kelvin–Voigt models, and nonlinear acoustic equations arising in ultrasound imaging, including the Jordan–Moore–Gibson–Thompson equation.

II

Stochastic inverse problems

Recovery of statistical properties of random sources or random potentials from correlation measurements, with particular interest in stochastic fractional Helmholtz equations.

III

Inverse potential scattering

Uniqueness and stability from multi-frequency scattering data.

IV

Physically motivated inverse problems

Moving-source identification and recovery of piecewise constant wave speeds in one-dimensional media, with broader interests in inverse problems in ocean acoustics, radiative transfer, astrophysics, and cosmology.
