Postdoctoral Researcher · School of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University, working with Prof. Xiang Xu. My research focuses on inverse problems for nonlinear hyperbolic equations, especially ultrasound imaging, inverse boundary value problems, stochastic inverse problems, and computational methods for the X-ray transform.
